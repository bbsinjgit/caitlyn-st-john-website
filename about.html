Caitlyn St John

Integrative psychotherapist, MSc, MUKCP

I’m originally from California and have made England my home for many years. I grew up close to someone working in psychotherapy, and have always been fascinated by how our earliest relationships shape who we are today.

Being invited into someone’s story, and walking alongside them through its ups and downs, feels like a real privilege. My experience includes time-limited therapy with students at the University of Leicester and longer-term work in private practice.

Outside the therapy room you’ll find me in the mountains or the woods, cooking, playing sport with my family, or over coffee with friends.
