Welcome, I’m Caitlyn.

I’m originally from California, and have made England my home for many years. I have recently moved to the countryside and I am enjoying and exploring the many benefits of immersing myself more in nature. I’m a psychotherapist with an MSc in Integrative Psychotherapy and a full member of the UK Council for Psychotherapy.

I was exposed to psychotherapy early in life, and I’ve been drawn ever since to the question of what makes us who we are, and why the same hurts or patterns of belief and behaviour can follow us through life.

Through my own long-term therapy, my training and my work with clients, I continue to learn about what forms us, and what keeps us feeling stuck in our grief, our relationships and our healing. I believe the relationships we have with ourselves and with those around us shape our wellbeing and our sense of meaning. Healing happens when we are met with warmth and kindness in our darkest places, when old pain is finally witnessed, when we begin to approach our story with curiosity, and when we discover that new ways of being with ourselves and others are possible.
