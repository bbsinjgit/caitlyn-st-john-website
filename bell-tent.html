Therapy can be found in many spaces.

As well as meeting online, you can choose to have your sessions in a cosy canvas bell tent, surrounded by the fields, woodland and birdsong of East Devon. You’re sheltered and comfortable inside, with the natural world all around you.

For some people, stepping out of a clinical room and into a quieter, greener space makes it easier to slow down, notice what they feel, and talk. The setting doesn’t replace the therapy. It is there to support it.
