Whenever you’re ready, I’m here.

Choosing a therapist can feel like a lot, whether it’s your first time or you’ve had therapy before. It’s completely normal to feel unsure or to have questions.

Send me a message or an email to arrange a free, no-pressure 15-minute online meeting. There’s no obligation and no need to decide on the spot. It’s simply a chance to see whether we’re a good fit.
