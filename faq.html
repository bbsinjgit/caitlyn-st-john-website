Frequently asked questions

Something not covered here? Ask me anything in a free 15-minute online meeting.

Contact
Where are you based?+

The bell tent is in the East Devon countryside, between Tipton St John and Newton Poppleford. It is a 12-minute drive from Sidmouth, and 25 minutes from Exeter city centre.

How often will we meet?+

Sessions are usually weekly, as this gives the best continuity for getting deep into the work and building our relationship. If weekly sessions aren’t possible or right for you, we can discuss other arrangements.

How many sessions will I need?+

Our contract with one another is open-ended. We will talk together about your goals and needs for therapy, and review how it is going.

Can I try in-person or walk and talk therapy and change my mind?+

Absolutely. Because I offer several options, we can try different things, or blend locations, to best suit your needs.
