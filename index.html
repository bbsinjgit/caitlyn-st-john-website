Come as you are.

Counselling and psychotherapy for young adults and adults, in East Devon and online.

Looking for a therapist can feel overwhelming, especially when life already does. I offer a non-judgemental space where you can be met with warmth and empathy, and begin to make sense of your story and the ways you feel stuck.

A place to be met in your pain, and seen.

People come to therapy for all sorts of reasons. Some are living with depression or anxiety. Some are grieving. Some want to understand themselves better, or feel stuck in ways they can’t quite shift. Others are struggling in their relationships, or living with the aftermath of trauma.

Whatever brings you, I would love to meet you just as you are and work together to step into more freedom and healing.
