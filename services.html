Walk & talk

Walk and talk therapy is a growing therapeutic service. It is simply having your therapy session while walking in nature alongside me, rather than sitting face to face.

There are many benefits to walk and talk therapy, offering something a little different from sitting in a room. Walking side by side can make it easier to be open and honest. Being in nature is grounding and calming for many people, and talking through life’s difficulties in a peaceful setting can help shift perspective and settle the body. The gentle, rhythmic movement of walking can also help you stay connected to what you’re feeling while you put it into words.

We’ll begin with a first meeting, either in my bell tent or online, to talk through whether walk and talk therapy feels right for you and to cover the practicalities. This isn’t about exercise, and we’ll go at whatever pace feels comfortable.

I’m happy to walk in all kinds of weather, safety permitting, and if you’d prefer to stay dry and settled, we can always meet in the bell tent instead. We’ll also talk about confidentiality, including what to do if we bump into someone you know.

There are many lovely walks from my bell tent. If you’d prefer a flatter route, we can meet elsewhere, such as in Tipton St John along the River Otter, or on one of the local commons.

Online

Online therapy became more familiar during the pandemic and can be a useful therapeutic option. My sessions are held on an online platform, and you can join from wherever is comfortable for you, within a confidential space.

Many people value the ease of online therapy, and the emotional distance of being physically separate can feel appealing to some.

The bell tent

My in-person sessions take place in a cosy canvas bell tent, surrounded by the fields, woodland and birdsong of East Devon. You’re sheltered and comfortable inside, with the natural world all around you. For some people, stepping out of a clinical room and into a quieter, greener space makes it easier to slow down, notice what they feel, and talk. The bell tent has countryside views, which can bring added calm and grounding.

There are many benefits to in-person therapy. For some, coming somewhere new, away from their own spaces, can offer more confidentiality, more safety and fewer distractions. Many prefer the greater sense of emotional connection that is possible when meeting face to face. The journey to and from sessions can also be a useful time of transition, with extra space to process.

Not sure which would suit you? We can talk it through in a free 15-minute online meeting.
